How to use this app

Simple guide · no tech degree required

Welcome. You only need your phone.

This is the free Guest Hub for Dani & Javad’s engagement party. No app store. No password. Just open a link (or scan a QR code) and join with your name.

Start here: Join Back to Home

1) Join once

Tap Join and type your first and last name. That’s it. The app remembers you on this phone so your points and stamps stick around.

2) Use the big tiles (or the bar at the bottom)

Home shows activities as colorful tiles. The bottom bar is a shortcut to the most used ones. You can leave any page and come back anytime.

  • Trivia — answer questions about the couple (points for correct answers).
  • Co-Mingle — talk to a real guest, type what they said, earn points.
  • Photo Wall — upload a phone photo or leave a short wish.
  • Leaderboard — see who’s winning.
  • Rings / Guest book — real-world party activities; check them off here.
  • Predictions, Jukebox, Stations, Margaritas, Advice — optional extras for fun.

3) QR codes around the room

Some signs open one activity directly (for example the margarita bar station). If a QR opens the wrong thing, just go Home and pick a tile.

4) Points & prize

Points add up while you play. New points stop at 4:30 PM (you can still open games after that). The winner is shown on Home / Leaderboard / the big screen. Follow the on-screen claim note.

5) Stuck? Try this

  • Refresh the page.
  • Use Join → “Resume saved profile” if you already joined on this phone.
  • If two people share a name, add a middle initial and create a new profile.
  • Photos need camera/gallery permission on your phone.
  • Still stuck? Find Javad or a helper with a smile (and maybe a taco).
