CALIFORNIA COMPETITIVE LOCAL EXCHANGE  CARRIERS

SUBJECT:

CANCELLATION  OF THE EXISTING COMPETITNE LOCAL
EXCHANGE CARRIER  REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Dear California Competitive Local  Exchange Carriers:

The Governor’s Office of Emergency Services (Cal OES) has conducted a review of all costs
associated with 911  services and has determined that Cal  OES  is  not required to pay the
Competitive Local Exchange Carriers for transmitting a customer’s 9-1-1  call to the 9-1-1  Selective
Router.  Given the State Emergency Telephone Number Account is diminishing, Cal OES is eliminating
all non-mandated costs.

As a courtesy, we are providing you with a 30-day notification of the cancellation of the
existing Competitive Local Exchange Carrier reimbursement policy. All current invoices
received  will be processed for payment. Invoices received more than thirty (30) days after the
date of this letter will  not be paid.

We  greatly  value  your  participation  to  provide  access  to  9-1-1  services  in  California.  If  you  have
questions,  please  feel  free  to  contact  Sofia  Long,  Cal  OES  Division  Chief,  CA  9-1-1  Branch,  at,
(916)657-9113.
