# Digest - Operations Manual Introduction

- California 9-1-1 system purpose: effective delivery of public emergency calls to appropriate PSAPs. 
- State role: oversight, standards, funding of network/database/call-taking equipment (local agencies still fund most staffing/facilities). 
- SETNA funds Branch program costs and eligible system components via surcharge mechanism (details and rates: live policy). 
- PSAPs: primary vs secondary; many small workstation counts. 
- County Coordinators designated per county for coordination/MSAG evolution. 
- Public-private partnership with vendors and associations (NENA, APCO). 

**Agent use:** mission framing only; do not invent call volume statistics as current facts.
