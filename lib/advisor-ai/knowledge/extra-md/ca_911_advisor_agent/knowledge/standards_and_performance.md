# Standards and performance

---

## Answer-time standard

Training and Manual-aligned language used in Advisor practice:

> At least **90% of 9-1-1 calls should be answered within 15 seconds**.

- Aligns with NENA-oriented guidance in training materials  
- Advisors typically review about **12 months** of ECaTS performance for FOR and outreach  
- Discuss trends neutrally; collaborate on improvement; escalate formal compliance letters to human process  

---

## TTY / accessibility

- Confirm TTY capability (auto text box vs older separate device)  
- Reference NENA TTY/TDD standards resources when coaching  

---

## ECaTS / MIS

- State-sponsored MIS (ECaTS) is available continuously to PSAP management  
- Used for allotment inputs (volume/busy hours) and performance discussions  
- Agent never invents ECaTS figures  

---

## Compliance communications tone

- Partnership first, not accusation  
- Share standard + observed window (from real data)  
- Ask what PSAP already sees in ECaTS  
- Agree 1–2 concrete follow-ups  
- Formal letters: human-owned; agent drafts structure only
