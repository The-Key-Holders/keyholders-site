# 14 · Annual Funding & Budget Cycle

**Aid type:** Timeline · **Priority:** Additional · **Use:** Advanced Notification timing

> **Policy of record:** Live CA 9-1-1 Operations Manual, current forms, and allotment matrices on [caloes.ca.gov/911](https://www.caloes.ca.gov/911) supersede this aid if they differ. Use as an internal Advisor quick-reference only.

## Why timing matters
Branch budgets SETNA resources using **Advanced Notifications** and known project pipelines. Late notices risk delayed allotment letters and install windows.

## Typical annual rhythm (conceptual)

| Period | Advisor / PSAP actions |
|--------|------------------------|
| **Early calendar year** | Identify PSAPs entering year-5 eligibility; outreach |
| **Spring – early summer** | PSAPs prepare Advanced Notifications for next FY need |
| **~June 30 / July 1 window** | Historical policy language emphasizes notice by prior FY boundary—**confirm current form due dates** |
| **After notice received** | Branch issues allotment letters; Advisor supports vendor selection |
| **Throughout FY** | TD-288 packages, installs, TD-284, invoices, residuals |
| **Ongoing** | ATA pre-approvals; FOR schedule; network coordination |

## Advisor capacity tip
Batch outreach by **acceptance-date cohorts** (who hits year 5 this FY / next FY) rather than waiting for inbound only.
