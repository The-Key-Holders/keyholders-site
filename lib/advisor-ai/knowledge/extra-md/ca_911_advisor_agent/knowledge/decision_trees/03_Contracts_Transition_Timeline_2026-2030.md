# 03 · California 9-1-1 Contracts & Transition Overview (2026–2030)

**Aid type:** Visual timeline · **Priority:** Highest · **Use:** Big-picture explanations for PSAPs & onboarding

> **Policy of record:** Live CA 9-1-1 Operations Manual, current forms, and allotment matrices on [caloes.ca.gov/911](https://www.caloes.ca.gov/911) supersede this aid if they differ. Use as an internal Advisor quick-reference only.

## Purpose
Show how **CPE MPA transition**, **NG9-1-1 network migration**, and **PSAP 5-year refresh waves** overlap — without implying exact calendar dates that change by PSAP.

## Phases (conceptual — confirm current Branch schedule)

| Phase | Window (approx.) | Advisor focus |
|-------|------------------|---------------|
| **A. Contract transition** | 2026–2027 | New/updated CPE MPA awareness; quote compliance; Cloud vs on-prem education; high Q volume |
| **B. Peak refresh + migration** | 2027–2028 | Advanced Notifications; residual planning; cutover coordination with network |
| **C. Steady-state NG operations** | 2028–2030 | i3-capable CPE norms; FOR findings on funding/CPE; year 6–7 maint vs refresh decisions |
| **Ongoing each FY** | Every year | Advanced Notification budgeting; ATA; FOR cycle; TD-290 / invoice processing |

## Per-PSAP overlay (always personalize)
1. Last **TD-284 acceptance date** → eligibility year  
2. Whether PSAP is on **Cloud** or **legacy on-prem**  
3. **NG9-1-1 deployment status** for county/region  
4. Open **TD-288** / residual window  

## Messaging guardrails
- Do **not** invent statewide cutover dates — use Branch dashboards / leadership communications.  
- Emphasize **PSAP-specific** eligibility over calendar year alone.  
- Contracts and network programs move on **different tracks** (Aid 02).
