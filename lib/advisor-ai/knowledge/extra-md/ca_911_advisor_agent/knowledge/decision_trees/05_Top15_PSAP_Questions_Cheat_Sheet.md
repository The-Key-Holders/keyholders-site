# 05 · Top 15 Most Common PSAP Questions & Responses

**Aid type:** Cheat sheet one-pager · **Priority:** Highest · **Use:** Live calls

> **Policy of record:** Live CA 9-1-1 Operations Manual, current forms, and allotment matrices on [caloes.ca.gov/911](https://www.caloes.ca.gov/911) supersede this aid if they differ. Use as an internal Advisor quick-reference only.

| # | PSAP question | Advisor response sketch |
|---|---------------|-------------------------|
| 1 | Are we eligible for a CPE replacement? | Eligibility is generally **5 years from last system acceptance (TD-284)**. Confirm acceptance date in ETS/PSAP file. If under 5 years, explain the cycle; if at/over 5, guide Advanced Notification. |
| 2 | How much funding will we get? | Allotment is based on **measured 9-1-1 call volume** (ECaTS) and the **CPE Fixed Allotment** methodology (funding levels 2–5). Use the calculator for estimates; **official amount is in the Branch allotment letter**. |
| 3 | What’s the difference between CPE and network funding? | **CPE** = call-handling equipment/system. **Network/NG9-1-1** = call delivery path. Different contracts and funding tracks (Aid 02). |
| 4 | Cloud or on-premise? | Both may be available via **lab-validated MPA vendors**. Cloud is generally NG9-1-1/i3 friendly. Encourage demos; do not mandate a brand. Share Aid 10. |
| 5 | What is Advanced Notification and when is it due? | PSAP form signaling intent to use CPE funds. Submit roughly **up to one year before** needed funding / eligibility. Supports Branch budgeting. Missing it can delay projects. |
| 6 | What is residual funding? | If complete system cost is **less than allotment**, the remainder is residual—only for **Branch-approved residual items**, planned with the project, usually quotes within **90 days of acceptance**. |
| 7 | Can residuals buy furniture / chairs / headsets? | Often **yes if on the approved residual list** and used in the 9-1-1 call-taking work area (workstation furniture tightly scoped; chairs limits apply). Confirm current Chapter III list. |
| 8 | Who pays the vendor—us or the State? | **Direct path (MPA):** contractor invoices the Branch after TD-284. **Reimbursement path:** PSAP pays then files TD-290. Prefer explaining both when PSAP is unsure. |
| 9 | What is TD-288? | **Commitment to Fund**—Branch approval of amount/items. Required before authorized spend; tracking number goes on invoices/claims. |
| 10 | What is TD-284? | **System Acceptance & Authorization**—PSAP certifies system accepted. Triggers payment path and residual timing clock. |
| 11 | Maintenance after 5 years? | Years **6–7** extended maintenance may be Branch-fundable with **pre-approval** (not deducted from next allotment per older policy text—confirm current). Year **8+** typically PSAP cost. Cloud may auto-continue month-to-month; refresh still recommended for residual opportunity. |
| 12 | Our CPE is damaged—will the State pay? | If **negligence / poor facility**: usually PSAP. If **act of nature** and reasonable protection: **case-by-case**. Aid 15. |
| 13 | Do we have to use the State MPA? | MPA is **not always mandatory**, but enables **direct funding** and competitive max pricing. Local procurement generally means **reimbursement** path. |
| 14 | How does NG9-1-1 affect our CPE choice? | Choose **lab-validated**, **NG-capable / i3-friendly** systems so network migration doesn’t force an early rip-and-replace. Coordinate cutovers (Aid 12). |
| 15 | What about training money (ATA)? | **Annual Training Allotment** is separate from CPE. Pre-approval required; TD-290 routing may differ. Don’t mix ATA into CPE residual without policy basis. |

## Call close checklist
1. Restate next action (form, demo, quote, escalation).  
2. Note PSAP code, date, and commitment in working log.  
3. Send follow-up email with form links / caloes.ca.gov/911.
