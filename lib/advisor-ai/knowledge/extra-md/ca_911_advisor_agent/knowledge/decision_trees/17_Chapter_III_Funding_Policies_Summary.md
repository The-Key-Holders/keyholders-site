# 17 · Key Chapter III Funding Policies Summary

**Aid type:** One-pager · **Priority:** Additional · **Use:** Policy questions on calls

> **Policy of record:** Live CA 9-1-1 Operations Manual, current forms, and allotment matrices on [caloes.ca.gov/911](https://www.caloes.ca.gov/911) supersede this aid if they differ. Use as an internal Advisor quick-reference only.

## Authority (short)
SETNA administration under **Revenue & Taxation Code** (Emergency Telephone Users Surcharge Act) + Warren 9-1-1 Act — Branch reimburses eligible equipment/services necessary to deliver/answer 9-1-1.

## Policy pillars (Chapter III map)

| Policy area | Core idea |
|-------------|-----------|
| New PSAP funding | Strict criteria (public agency, call volume, 24×7, standards, wireless/text/NG commitments, TDe-280) |
| Agency 9-1-1 expenses | Eligible activities/expenses with pre-approval; reimbursement process |
| Education funding | Training/education rules; ATA |
| Network services | Eligible network services; direct vs reimb; coordination on moves |
| **CPE funding** | Fixed allotment; Advanced Notification; residuals; Y6–7 maint; repairs case-by-case |
| **CPE provisioning** | Call-volume levels; busy month/hour methods; ECaTS required |
| GIS funding | Separate allotment & authorized uses |
| Processes | Direct vs reimbursement for CPE, GIS, network, agency expenses |

## CPE fixed allotment — five bullets
1. Equitable funding via **call volume** + standard system pricing concepts  
2. **MPA** provides max prices & direct-pay path (not always mandatory)  
3. **Advanced Notification** for budgeting & planning  
4. **Residuals** only for approved incremental items with timing rules  
5. **Y6–7** maint optional with pre-approval; **Y8+** generally local
