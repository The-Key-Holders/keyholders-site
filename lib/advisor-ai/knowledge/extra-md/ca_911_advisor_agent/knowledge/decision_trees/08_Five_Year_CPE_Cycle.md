# 08 · 5-Year CPE Funding Cycle & Refresh Rules

**Aid type:** Timeline / table · **Priority:** Supporting · **Use:** Planning conversations

> **Policy of record:** Live CA 9-1-1 Operations Manual, current forms, and allotment matrices on [caloes.ca.gov/911](https://www.caloes.ca.gov/911) supersede this aid if they differ. Use as an internal Advisor quick-reference only.

## Lifecycle (from system acceptance)

| Year | Status | Funding notes |
|------|--------|---------------|
| **0** | Install + acceptance (**TD-284**) | Warranty typically begins; allotment applied to project |
| **1** | Warranty / early maintenance | Included in standard system package terms |
| **2–4** | Maintenance years | Covered under project maintenance structure |
| **5** | **Refresh eligibility** | Advanced Notification window; full replacement or certified upgrade |
| **6–7** | Extended maintenance option | May be Branch-funded with **pre-approval**; **not** deducted from fixed allotment (per Ch. III text—confirm current) |
| **8+** | Beyond extended window | Maintenance **generally PSAP responsibility** |

## Cloud note (regional PSAP materials)
Cloud CPE extended maintenance past initial **60 months** may continue **month-to-month** until next refresh. Refresh after 60 months is **recommended** to update equipment and unlock **residual** opportunity.

## Certified upgrade alternative
Where existing CPE is **certified to function as new for an additional five years**, a certified upgrade path may apply under CPE funding policy—confirm with current MPA/policy language.

## Advisor prompts
1. “What is your TD-284 acceptance date?”  
2. “Are you planning refresh at year 5 or riding extended maint?”  
3. “If year 6–7, have you requested pre-approval ≥ ~2 months before agreement ends?”
