Reimbursement Policy:

1. Educational Materials: A TD-290 Reimbursement Claim form is submitted by the PSAP to the appropriate 9-1-1 Office Consultant. The 9-1-1 Office Consultant will:
   1. Make sure that the TD-288 Commitment to Fund and proof of payment is included with the 290.
      1. If the appropriate paperwork is attached, the 9-1-1 Office Consultant will log in the database the date the 290 is received and give the package to the Reimbursement Review Committee for final approval/completion.
      2. If the appropriate paperwork is not attached, the 9-1-1 Office Consultant will fill out a Reimbursement Claim Dispute Notification form and return the 290/paperwork to the PSAP noting the discrepancies.
2. CALNENA-Sponsored Events: A TD-290 Reimbursement Claim form is submitted by the PSAP to the appropriate 9-1-1 Office Consultant. The 9-1-1 Office Consultant will:
   1. Make sure that the PSAP’s travel/per diem policy is attached, receipts, and any other documentation that is required. Note that if the PSAP’s travel policy is not attached nor referenced, the State’s travel policy will prevail. The 9-1-1 Office Consultant will log in the database the date the 290 is received and give the package to the Reimbursement Review Committee for final approval/completion.
   2. If the appropriate paperwork is not attached, the 9-1-1 Office Consultant will fill out a Reimbursement Claim Dispute Notification form and return the 290/paperwork to the PSAP noting the discrepancies.
3. CPE/GIS Equipment/Services: A TD-290 Reimbursement Claim form is submitted by the PSAP to the appropriate 9-1-1 Office Consultant on those rare times that a PSAP does pay for 9-1-1 equipment/services on their own. The 9-1-1 Office Consultant will:
   1. Make sure that the TD-288 Commitment to Fund and proof of payment is included with the 290.
      1. If the appropriate paperwork is attached, the 9-1-1 Office Consultant will log in the database the date the 290 is received, confirm the 288 (pre-approval) matches the reimbursement claim information, then sign the recommend approval portion of the 290 give it to the 9-1-1 Office Consultant’s supervisor for final approval.
      2. Once the 9-1-1 Office Consultant’s supervisor signs/approves the 290, the original is given to the 9-1-1 Fiscal Administration Unit for processing for payment.
