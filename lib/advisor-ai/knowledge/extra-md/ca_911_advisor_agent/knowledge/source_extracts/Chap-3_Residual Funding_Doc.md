**CalOES CA 911 Emergency Communications Branch**

**CA 911 Operations Manual – Chapter III – Funding**

**URL:** [www.caloes.ca.gov/911](http://www.caloes.ca.gov/911)

If the final cost for a complete 9-1-1 system CPE replacement is less than the PSAP’s CPE funding fixed allotment eligibility amount, the remaining amount is referred to as residual funds. These residual funds may be used for the purchase of additional CA 9- 1-1 Branch approved equipment and services as detailed in the Service/Equipment Approval List for Residual Funds provided in the CA 9-1-1 Branch Operations Manual, Chapter 3 (Funding).

All quotes and/or POs for use of residual funds must be submitted to the CA 9-1-1 Branch no later than 90 days after CPE system acceptance. TD-288 approved purchases must be invoiced, and where required, a TD-290 Reimbursement Claim must be submitted, within twelve (12) months of system acceptance. For further clarification of this requirement, please contact your assigned CA 9-1-1 Branch Advisor.

**Service/Equipment Approval List for Residual Funds**

A PSAP may request pre-approval from the CA 9-1-1 Branch to use residual funds from their CPE funding allotment in accordance with requirements outlined in the CPE FUNDING POLICY (found in the CA 9-1-1 Branch Operations Manual, Chapter-3, Funding) to purchase additional 9-1-1 services and/or equipment from the list below where the services and/or equipment will be used within the 9-1-1 communication center (including the 9-1-1 equipment room) and in direct support of the delivery of 9-1-1 traffic to the 9-1-1 call taker.

**Funding for the items below must be requested at the time of, or during, CPE system replacement.**

1. **Additional 9-1-1 Call-handling Workstation Position:**

(After primary CPE procured)

1. Desktop or laptop computer;

2. Desktop monitor, minimum 22” - maximum 50”;

3. Mouse;

4. Standard Keyboard;

5. Programmable Auxiliary Keypad Dialer;

6. IP Phone Set (only if requested by the PSAP);

7. Workstation Arbitrator;

8. Instant Recall Recorder;

9. Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS)/Workstation Positions; (15) minutes;

10. Audio Integration Device (Headset Box);

**Back Room Equipment:**

1. Redundant UPSs capable of providing backup power to all Contractor supplied backroom equipment for two (2) hours;
2. Interface to accommodate the delivery of administrative/ring down/etc. calls, with Caller ID where applicable;
3. Capability for third party contractor to collect real time CDR Data via IP connection;
4. Cabling from local demarcation point at the PSAP to PSAPs backroom equipment if necessary;
5. Cabling from backroom equipment to all call handling workstations;
6. Dynamic interface to Computer Aided Dispatch (CAD), on premise Logging Recorder, Radio, Geographical Information Systems (GIS), Time Synchronization (Netclock), etc.;
7. Redundant Routers for connectivity to RNSP/PNSP
8. Redundant Firewalls.
9. Additional items from “Cloud/Data Center Based 9-1-1 System” as defined in Chap-3, Funding;
10. **GIS** services, software, and equipment;
11. **Mapping** monitors. One (1) per workstation and/or one (1) large wall display;
12. **Project Management Professional** (PMP)/Emergency Number Professional (ENP) Certified Professional Consulting services for 9-1-1 CPE replacement;
13. **Furniture**. 9-1-1 call-taker workstations only. No mobile or detached storage.
14. **Dispatcher Chairs**. Maximum (3) chairs per workstation;
15. **Headsets**;
16. **Logging recorder** for 9-1-1 calls;
17. **Temporary relocation** of 9-1-1 CPE;
18. **Pre-Arrival Instruction** System;
19. **IP Phone** Handset;
20. **Reader Boards**.
