New Public Safety Answering Point (PSAP) funding policy/criteria is outlined in the CA State Operations Manual-Chapter III, Pages 4-5.  Link provided below.

Approval for CA State 911 SETNA funding for “new” PSAP “start-up” is based on agency 911 call-volume received within agency’s jurisdiction; minimum 1,201 or more calls per month.  Funding will be authorized at level needed for PSAP to answer 911 calls at a P.01 grade of service.

State will provide funding to establish a standard turn-key 911 system, based on vendor price quote for Customer Premise Equipment (CPE) positions and required network circuits (911 Trunks, ANI/ALI, ALT Answer PSAP.  Reference CA State Operations Manual-Chapter III, Page 18.
