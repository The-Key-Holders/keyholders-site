**Pre-approval Process**

The 9-1-1 Office pre-approval process will vary depending on the nature of the request, method of payment, and type of State contract used (if any). The Commitment to Fund 9-1-1 Equipment and Services form (TD-288) is the main vehicle used by consultants to grant pre-approval to PSAPs for equipment and services with the exception of Network requests (see below). NOTE: For pre-approval requests related to educational materials, meetings, County Coordinator or wireless activities, assign tracking number and forward to Reimbursement Administrator.

CPE/GIS

Prior to approving any allotment spending (i.e. CPE, GIS) the consultant should verify the PSAP’s availability of funds:

1. Eligibility
   1. Has it been 5 years since PSAP’s last CPE upgrade/replacement?
   2. Does PSAP have a Letter of Agency on file as required for GIS?
2. Prior Spending
   1. Check PSAP folder, white binder, and database for documentation of prior allotment spending and deduct from available balance.
