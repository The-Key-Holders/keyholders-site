**CPE Project (Red) Folder Closeout Process**

Once CPE invoice payment has been authorized, and all residual spending approvals have been paid, project (Red) folder may be closed and contents archived in the “Library” White Binders. The 9-1-1 Emergency Telephone Systems (ETS) Database requires update as progress occurs, so PSAP Record would have had current equipment information entered when TD-284 CPE System Acceptance Form received, and all approvals associated with the CPE Allotment use complete with required details and assigned “Closed” status.

1. Remove contents from red folder and order as follows:
   1. CPE Purchase Checklist
   2. Reverse chronology of CPE purchase documentation:
      1. Vendor Invoice (with payment authorization date and signature)
      2. TD-284 CPE System Acceptance Form
      3. TD-288 Approval of CPE Purchase
      4. PSAP Purchase Order
      5. CPE Price Quote
      6. CPE Scope of Work
   3. CPE Allotment Residual Spending Approval Documentation
   4. Correspondence/Documentation from Left side of folder:
      1. “Footprint” (allotment balance tracking ledger)
      2. TD-285 CPE Allotment Spending Plan
      3. CPE Allotment Preapproval Letter
      4. Advance Notification Form
      5. Call stats evaluation
   5. Purge irrelevant documents (e.g. original SOWs/Quotes subsequently revised)
2. Archive the documentation in PSAP’s Archive (White) Binder:
   1. Prior CPE replacement project documentation now irrelevant and may be purged
   2. Do not purge other historical documentation (e.g. Comm-280 Forms; Network Orders)
