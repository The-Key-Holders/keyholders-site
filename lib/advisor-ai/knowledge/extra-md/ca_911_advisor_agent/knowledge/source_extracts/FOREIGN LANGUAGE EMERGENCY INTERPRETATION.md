**FOREIGN LANGUAGE EMERGENCY INTERPRETATION**

CA 9-1-1 Policy on foreign language is located in the State 9-1-1 Operations Manual, Chapter VII: <http://www.caloes.ca.gov/PublicSafetyCommunicationsSite/Documents/008-ChapterVII.pdf>

**FUNDING**

PSAP may utilize any contractor of choice. PSAP is responsible to provide the service, State 911 Branch assists with funding. Reference statement at end of Chapter VII which allows the CA 9-1-1 Branch to do direct funding when a PSAP uses a California state contract. Currently the State recognizes two state contracts in this manner, our CA 9-1-1 contract (currently- Language Line Solutions) and CMAS contracts:

“*Foreign language interpretation services for 9-1-1 calls may be directly billed to the CA 9-1-1 Branch when these services are provided by a California state contract. All other service arrangements will be funded on a reimbursement basis as defined in Chapter III, Funding”*.

Note: we only pay up the current contract rate of $.59 per minute.
