**Instructions on how to complete an Employee Training Request (ETR)**

**Employee Training Request (ETR)** - Once you access Lotus Notes, at the top of the screen click on File – Application – Open.

![](data:image/png;base64...)

A new window will appear, in the “Look in” drop down section find “OES HQ1 OES.”

![](data:image/png;base64...)

Scroll to the bottom and double click the file folder labeled “Training.”

![](data:image/png;base64...)

Once in the Training folder, double click the “Training & Staff Development” database.

![](data:image/png;base64...)

At the top of the screen click the “Create Employee Training Request” button and select the appropriate training type.

![](data:image/png;base64...)

Once you select the appropriate training, proceed to fill out all of the categories in the training request to include:

* Training Type
* Reservation Made By
* Provider
* Category of Training
* Dates/Hours
* Cost (if any)
* Etc.

![](data:image/png;base64...)

Once completed save and send for approval.

If you have questions, please contact Michele Williams, Training Coordinator for PSC at (916) 657-9393.
