**Fiscal and Operational Review Process**

**Advisor Folder Preparation Instructions**

This set of instructions is intended to provide the State 9‑1‑1 Emergency Communications Branch Advisor with guidelines for preparing the folder associated with the Fiscal and Operations Review (FOR) process for each PSAP.

All of the templates for the various sections of the binder are stored in the 9-1-1 Advisors\_Fiscal & Operational Review (FOR) folder. Each template should be customized for each PSAP and saved in the PSAP “FOR Archive” folder as a new file labeled by “year” of FOR meeting. Passages that are highlighted in blue require some sort of attention during the binder preparation process.

The FOR folder is separated into six (6) sections as listed below and the guidelines for preparing each section are described further in this document:

Fiscal and Operational Review Summary

Section I – Fiscal Review

Section II – Network Review

Section III – Customer Premise Equipment (CPE)

Section IV – Operational Performance

Section V – Next Generation 9-1-1 and Cloud-CPE

Section VI - References

**Cover Sheet**

A template for a cover sheet for the folder is provided with the rest of the templates in the “Fiscal and Operations Review” folder. Insert the name and date of the review.

**Fiscal and Operational Review Summary**

While this is the first document of the folder, it will provide an overview of the preliminary findings and issues that the Advisor expects to discussed during the review meeting. *This section should be prepared after Sections 1 through 6 have been completed.* Various portions of the template include places where the Advisor can provide specific information to the PSAP Management.

**Section I - Fiscal Review**

This section involves using an Excel spreadsheet where various tabs require input and all the information is summarized on the first tab titled “Summary Report”. Required input for each of the tabs is described below.

**Summary** tab – Information from the other tabs automatically updates this tab, so the only input required here is to pick the PSAP from the pull down list in cell B3. *However, the bottom line of this page may need to be adjusted for unique circumstances (see “Five Year Estimate” below).*

**CPE** tab – The Advisor will investigate the PSAP History File and enter information from the last CPE upgrade (change-out, Refresh). Entries should be broken into one of three categories: CPE and Maintenance, Extended Maintenance, or Residual Spending.

**ATA** tab – Include all approved expenditures from the Annual Training Allotment (ATA) for the current fiscal year.

**Network Expenses** tab – Temporarily, this will not be included until we can determine how we are going to capture this information.

This one requires research into the ETS database as well as some assumptions about the data. First, in ETS, identify all of the billing numbers that reflect payments made by the State during the time period being measured (usually the past fiscal year) and enter the amounts for the specific months where data is available. Also enter the circuit ID and type of circuit in the far right column to be used as the Consultant prepares Section 4 of the binder. (Note: do not include payments made to selective router (SR) type circuit IDs as these charges are usually the cost of ALI database charges and are billed only to the county Sheriff for the entire county.)

Once the matrix is populated will all of the ETS data that is available, The Consultant will have to enter data where other data wasn’t available in ETS. Example: if the ETS data indicates payments for 10 months out of the past fiscal year at $125.00 per month, but there is not record of payment for the other two months, it is reasonable to enter the same amount for the two months where ETS did not pick up the payment.

**Reimbursement** tab – List all reimbursements for the past fiscal year that do not fall into the ATA category on this table. The total will be displayed on this tab and on the Summary sheet.

**Foreign Language** tab – Foreign language interpretation services are provided by Language Line and they track the costs and usage by PSAP, for any given period. This information can be obtained from the current state contractor/state project manager.

**MIS Costs** tab – This tab has been deleted. The cost of ECaTS is $141.00 per PSAP + $79.00 x 12 months + $2,640.00 and has been manually added in on the Summary page. ($79.00 is $36,000.00 divided by 459 PSAPs to give you per PSAP amount)

**Section II - Network Review**

This section includes an overview statement of what network services are provided by the phone company that provides the 9-1-1 network services. The Advisor will request from the network provider a Customer record document that provide the network information, CPE information (if provided by the network provider), star codes, etc. A list of all network services will need to be included in this section for the PSAP in order to provide pertinent data for discussion. This discussion is important because it is the only way to ensure that the billing from the telephone company is accurate.

Note: If discrepancies are discovered (i.e. BTNs inaccurately assigned to the wrong PSAP), the ETS database should be corrected by someone in the Fiscal Administration Unit.

**Section III - CPE and Maintenance Review**

This section provides an overview of the nature of CPE and maintenance funding from the CA 9-1-1 Branch. The Advisor should include a copy of the TD288 CPE/Maintenance procurement documents for use as verification and discussion during the review meeting.

**Section IV - Operational Performance**

The operational Performance is of particular interest to the public and substandard performance should be discussed with the PSAP management and the discussions should be documented.

In order to determine the average speed of answer for the PSAP, the Advisor will have to research the answer times for the PSAP as reflected in ECaTS. The State of California standard (and the NENA Standard) for answering 9‑1‑1 calls is at least 90% of their 9‑1‑1 calls should be answered within 15 seconds. It is recommended that the Advisor review the answer times for the past 12 months of operations and provide the PSAP with the average for that period.

TTY compliance should include a visual inspection by the Advisor of the PSAPs TTY capability. This will usually involve observing a call taker demonstrate how the equipment automatically answers TTY calls by providing a text box on the call taker’s screen. Older solutions may include a separate TTY device that requires the call taker to turn it on for each TTY call. A link to the NENA website is provided for the PSAP to download the TTY/TDD Standards as needed.

The State sponsored MIS system ECaTS should be discussed to be sure that the PSAP management understands that this resource is available to them on a continuous basis.

**Section V – Next Generation 9-1-1 and Cloud-CPE**

The CA 9‑1‑1 Branch has been actively working with PSAPs and vendors to install Next Generation 9-1-1 (NG 9-1-1) equipment at all PSAPs within the State of California.

Cloud-based CPE is the new NENA i3 compliant call taking equipment that has been validated in the NG 9-1-1 Lab and is approved by the state of California for all PSAPs. During the FOR the advisor will go over all approved vendors that are currently available to sell their equipment to PSAPs in the state and answer any funding questions the PSAP may have.
