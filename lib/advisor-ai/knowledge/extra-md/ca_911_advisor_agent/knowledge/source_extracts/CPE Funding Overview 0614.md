**FUNDING PROCESS - 9‑1‑1 System CPE Replacement or Certified Upgrade**

**Direct Funding Process - 9‑1‑1 System CPE Replacement or Certified Upgrade**

In accordance with the *CPE FUNDING POLICY* outlined in this chapter (pgs. III.10-III.13), PSAPs are required to follow the 9-1-1 System CPE Direct Funding Process when seeking State CA 9-1-1 Branch funding for a complete 9‑1‑1 system CPE replacement or certified upgrade where the PSAP uses the current State 9-1-1 CPE Master Purchase Agreement (MPA) contracts or California Multiple Award Schedule (CMAS) agreement. Invoices will be billed by the contractor to the CA 9-1-1 Branch for direct payment. PSAPs must follow their respective agency’s procurement processes when issuing a purchase order (PO) to procure 9‑1‑1 CPE systems.

1. **PSAP Requests Funding**

The PSAP submits an ADVANCE NOTIFICATION FOR CPE FUNDING form (Form is currently being finalized) to the CA 9-1-1 Branch by June 30, one-year before the funding is needed. Once the form has been finalized, it will be posted on the CA 9-1-1 Branch website and may be viewed or downloaded from the CA 9-1-1 Branch website as follows:

<http://www.caloes.ca.gov/PSC/Pages/Publications/Forms.aspx>

1. **CA 9-1-1 Branch Issues “Pre‑Authorization Letter”**

If the PSAP request meets the funding requirements outlined in this chapter, a “pre**‑**authorization letter” will be sent to the PSAP within eight (8) weeks of the date the ADVANCE NOTIFICATION FOR CPE FUNDING form was received by the CA 9-1-1 Branch and will detail the approved CPE funding fixed allotment amount. The pre‑authorization letter will also detail the steps the PSAP must follow to obtain final purchase approval from the CA 9-1-1 Branch.

1. **PSAP Chooses 9**‑**1**‑**1 CPE System**

Once pre‑authorization is obtained from the CA 9-1-1 Branch, the PSAP is encouraged to examine the functional differences of available 9‑1‑1 CPE systems, determine each contractor’s ability to provide different services, and obtain multiple price offers from contractors for systems with identical configurations and functionality needed by the PSAP. A copy of the State’s MPA User’s Instructions may be viewed or downloaded from the CA 9-1-1 Branch website as follows:

<http://www.caloes.ca.gov/PSC/Pages/Services/911/911Systems-and-Services-Contractor-Information.aspx>

Prices on the MPA contracts are the maximum allowable. PSAPs may be able to obtain additional price discounts and/or additional services and equipment (see *Incremental Service/Equipment Approval List* (pg. III.13) in this chapter) by obtaining price offers from different contractors.

The PSAP can compare their price quotes against current maximum contract prices for each CPE contractor that may be viewed or downloaded from the CA 9-1-1 Branch website as follows:

<http://www.caloes.ca.gov/PSC/Pages/Services/911/911Systems-and-Services-Contractor-Information.aspx>

1. **Contractor Prepares CPE Statement Of Work (SOW)**

The selected contractor prepares a CPE SOW (pgs. III.30-III.31) detailing all deliverables for the installation. Please refer to *CPE* *STATEMENT OF WORK* at the end of this chapter. This document is important because it insures that both the PSAP and the contractor agree on the elements necessary to meet the needs of the PSAP.

NOTE: Coordination for 9-1-1 CPE system moves and/or system replacement that impact 9-1-1 network services must be coordinated with all stakeholders including the PSAP manager,

CA 9-1-1 Branch consultant, equipment contractor, and network provider(s).

1. **PSAP Prepares and Submits a PO Package**

The CA 9-1-1 Branch will assist the PSAP, as needed, in preparing a PO package. The PO document shall serve as the legally binding procurement agreement between the PSAP and the contractor. The PO package must include the following information.

* 1. Statement of Work (SOW)
  2. itemized list of all system elements
  3. unsigned PO, which must include:
     1. the “ship to” address where the system will be installed;
     2. the “bill to” address of the CA 9-1-1 Branch for all applicable system elements approved for funding;
     3. a statement on the front of the PO stating, *“The purchase, installation, and maintenance of items identified in this purchase order shall comply exclusively with the terms and conditions of (current 9‑1‑1 CPE MPA or CMAS contract number) and the attached STATEMENT OF WORK.”*
     4. a summary of costs on the PO document for the standard system replacement or certified upgrade including equipment, installation, training, maintenance, and taxes;
     5. a summary of costs on the PO document, in addition to the standard system, for other items on the contract the PSAP requests to purchase at the same time (list CPE allotment items separately from GIS allotment items); and

Completed CPE ALLOTMENT SPENDING PLAN (TDe‑285) form. The TDe‑285 form may be viewed or downloaded from the CA 9-1-1 Branch website as follows:

<http://www.caloes.ca.gov/PSC/Pages/Publications/Forms.aspx>

1. **CA 9-1-1 Branch Issues a Commitment To Fund**

The CA 9-1-1 Branch will review the PO package. If the PSAP’s PO package meets the funding requirements outlined in this chapter, a COMMITMENT TO FUND (TDe**‑**288) form detailing the approved amount will be sent to the PSAP within four (4) weeks of the date the CA 9-1-1 Branch received a complete and accurate PO package. If the PSAP’s PO package does not meet the funding requirements, the assigned CA 9-1-1 Branch consultant will contact the PSAP for further information within two (2) weeks of the date the CA 9-1-1 Branch received the PO package.

1. **PSAP Authorizes Contractor To Proceed With Purchase and Installation**

Upon receipt of the approved TDe‑288, the PSAP may then sign the PO and authorize the contractor to proceed with purchase and installation. The PSAP and the contractor should closely examine the schedule included in the SOW prior to signing the document. The PSAP must provide the contractor the signed PO.

1. **PSAP Performs Acceptance Testing**

Acceptance testing must be performed in accordance with contractual provisions. Once a system passes the acceptance testing, the PSAP shall submit a signed 9‑1‑1 Service and equipment SYSTEM ACCEPTANCE AND AUTHORIZATION (TDe‑284) form to the CA 9-1-1 Branch so that payment can be rendered to the contractor. A copy of the TDe-284 form may be viewed or downloaded from the CA 9-1-1 Branch website as follows:

<http://www.caloes.ca.gov/PSC/Pages/Publications/Forms.aspx>

1. **Contractor Invoices the CA 9-1-1 Branch**

Upon satisfactory installation of equipment or service and PSAP acceptance, where applicable, the contractor may invoice the CA 9-1-1 Branch. When invoicing the CA 9-1-1 Branch, the contractor must include a breakdown of applicable equipment, labor, taxes, and surcharges. The tracking number from the TDe‑288 form must be noted on the invoice. When creating an account name for an invoice, the contractor must use the account naming convention provided by the CA 9-1-1 Branch on the TDe‑288 form.
