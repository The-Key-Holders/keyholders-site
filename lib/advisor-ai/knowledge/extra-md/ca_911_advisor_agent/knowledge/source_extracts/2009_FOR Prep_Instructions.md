**Fiscal and Operational Review Process**

**Consultant’s Binder Preparation Instructions**

This set of instructions is intended to provide the State 9‑1‑1 Emergency Communications Office Consultants with guidelines for preparing the binder associated with the Fiscal and Operations Review process for each PSAP.

All of the templates for the various sections of the binder are stored on the “O” drive in the “Fiscal and Operations Review” folder. Each of these templates should be customized for each PSAP and saved in a separate folder on the server in the “PSAP FOR Binders” folder. Passages that are highlighted in blue require some sort of attention during the binder preparation process.

The binder is separated into 10 sections as listed below and the guidelines for preparing each section are described further in this document.

Section 1 – Executive Summary

Section 2 – Contact Information

Section 3 – Fiscal Review

Section 4 – Network Review

Section 5 – CPE (including maintenance)

Section 6 – Operational Issues (speed of answer, 24/7 operation, TTY, MIS)

Section 7 – GIS and Wireless Calls

Section 8 – Funding Sources

Section 9 – NENA Operational Standards

Section 10 – Findings

**Note: One of the first steps in preparing the F.O.R. binder should be to solicit network information from the serving telephone company for comparison with the ETS database information.**

**Cover Sheet**

A template for a cover sheet for the binder is provided with the rest of the templates in the “Fiscal and Operations Review” folder. Insert the name and date of the review.

**Section 1 – Executive Summary**

While this is the first section of the binder, it will provide an overview of the preliminary findings and issues that the Consultant expects to discussed during the review meeting. Therefore, this section should be prepared after Sections 2 through 9 have been completed. Various portions of the template include places where the Consultant can provide specific information to the PSAP Management.

**Section 2 – Contact Information**

This template provides a standard format for all contact information and requires some research, possibly including searching for contact information on the internet. The section at the bottom that briefly describes the network services funded by the State will have to be done after Section 4 (Network Review) has been completed, including matching up the ETS data with the data provided by the serving telephone company.

**Section 3 – Fiscal Review**

This section includes an MS Word document that describes the various categories that are analyzed. It also involves using an Excel spreadsheet where various tabs require input and all the information is summarized on the first tab titled “Summary Report”. Required input for each of the tabs is described below.

**Summary** tab – Information from the other tabs automatically updates this tab, so the only input required here is to pick the PSAP from the pull down list in cell B3. However, the bottom line of this page may need to be adjusted for unique circumstances (see “Five Year Estimate” below).

**CPE** tab – The Consultant will investigate the PSAP binder and enter information from the last CPE upgrade or change out. Entries should be broken into one of three categories: CPE, Maintenance, or Residual Spending.

**GIS** tab – Again, the records in the PSAP binder will reflect the amount of funding that is (or was) available to the PSAP for GIS implementation and the amount that has been provided from this fund. Enter the various components and the respective value in the space provided and the Summary tab will be automatically populated.

**CAA** tab – Include all approved expenditures from the CAA allotment for the current fiscal year. The spreadsheet will calculate the balance available for the current fiscal year.

**Network Expenses** tab – This one requires research into the ETS database as well as some assumptions about the data.

First, in ETS, identify all of the billing numbers that reflect payments made by the State during the time period being measured (usually the past fiscal year) and enter the amounts for the specific months where data is available. Also enter the circuit ID and type of circuit to be used as the Consultant prepares Section 4 of the binder. (Note: do not include payments made to selective router (SR) type circuit IDs as these charges are usually the cost of ALI database charges and are billed only to the county Sheriff for the entire county.) One of the Fiscal Management staff [Carolyn’s group] will assist with this portion.

Once the matrix is populated with all of the ETS data that is available, The Consultant will have to enter data where other data wasn’t available in ETS. Example: if the ETS data indicates payments for 10 months out of the past fiscal year at $125.00 per month, but there is not record of payment for the other two months, it is reasonable to enter the same amount for the two months where ETS did not pick up the payment.

**ALI Database** tab – Again, one of the Fiscal Management staff [Carolyn’s group] will assist with this calculation. The ALI Database funding is made by county so in order to calculate the PSAP’s proportional amount this is a multi-step process:

1st – In the “Proportion” tab, ensure that the number of positions (column F) properly reflects the positions deployed (not just funded by the State).

2nd – In the ETS database, find “Selective Routing” among the list of PSAPs and extract the data for the period being addressed. Alternatively, the selective routing billing information may be found under the county Sheriff’s Office PSAP charges (look for “SR” in the circuit ID). [Hint: According to AT&T, selective router charges are usually billed as follows: (County area code)-911-99(2 digit county code). For Sacramento County, the selective router billing would be under 916-911-9934.]

3rd – Enter the amounts for the period being measured (typically the last fiscal year) in the space provided on the ALI Database tabbed spreadsheet.

The build-in formulas will calculate the appropriate proportion of ALI database funding for this PSAP.

**Reimbursement** tab – List all reimbursements for the past fiscal year that do not fall into the CAA category on this table. The total will be displayed on this tab and on the Summary sheet.

**MSAG** tab – This is another reimbursement that is made on a “per county” basis. Research the PSAP binder for the county coordinator for this county and list all reimbursed costs in the table on this tab. The appropriate proportion of the county’s costs will be calculated and listed on this tab and on the Summary tab.

**MIS Costs** tab –The appropriate annual amount should be calculated and displayed on this page and on the Summary tab page automatically once the PSAP name is chosen from the pick list at the top of the Summary tab.

**Foreign Lang** tab – Foreign language translation services are provided by Network Omni and they track the costs, by PSAP, for any given period. Consultants will be provided with access to the Network Omni database via the internet. Query the database for this PSAP for the appropriate time period. [Until the database is available to Consultants on-line, Carolyn Brown can request the required information directly from her contact at Network Omni.]

The **five year estimate** that is calculated in the last line of the Summary tab page may need to be adjusted for unique circumstances. For example, calculations for network services may include one-time initiation costs that will not be required in future years. Modifications to this calculation should be made in order to provide the most accurate estimate of the fiscal support that will be provided by the state.

**Section 4 – Network Review**

This section includes an overview statement of the State funded network services deployed at the PSAP and the Consultant will have to copy and paste some information from the “Network Expenses” tab of the Fiscal Review worksheet in order to provide pertinent data for discussion. This discussion is important because it is the only way to ensure that the billing from the telephone company is accurate.

**Section 5 CPE (Including Maintenance)**

This section provides an overview of the nature of the CPE and maintenance funding from the State. The Consultant should include a copy of the SOW in this section which will be used for verification and discussion during the review meeting.

**Section 6 Operational Issues**

The operational issues are of particular interest to the public and substandard performance should be discussed with the PSAP management and the discussions should be documented.

In order to determine the average speed of answer for the PSAP, the Consultant will have to research the answer times for the PSAP as reflected in CARSNet or in FRNIS. The State of California standard (and the NENA Standard) for answering 9‑1‑1 calls is at least 90% of their 9‑1‑1 calls should be answered within 10 seconds. It is recommended that the Consultant review the answer times for the past 12 months of operations and provide the PSAP with the average for that period. Also, the Consultant will have to calculate the average monthly call volume during the same period.

24/7 operation can be verified through FRNIS or CARSNet by simply looking at the calls per hour for the past month.

TTY compliance should include a visual inspection by the Consultant of the PSAPs TTY capability. This will usually involve observing a call taker demonstrate how the equipment automatically answers TTY calls by providing a text box on the call taker’s screen. Older solutions may include a separate TTY device that requires the call taker to turn it on for each TTY call. Also included in the binder are a number of NENA Operational Standards regarding TTY that discuss training and testing standards that should be discussed with the PSAP management.

The State sponsored MIS system applicable to the PSAP should be discussed (FRNIS or CARSNet) to be sure that the PSAP management understands that this resource is available to them on a continuous basis.

**Section 7 GIS and Wireless Calls**

Information regarding the status of funding for GIS will have been collected as the Consultant completed the Fiscal Review. The status of implementation of wireless call taking should be discussed and documented during the F.O.R. meeting.

**Section 8 – Funding Sources**

This section provides references to various federal web sites and does not require input from the Consultant other than to make sure the PSAP management understands that it is part of the binder.

**Section 9 NENA Operations Standards**

As section 8 above, this section provides information that requires no further input from the Consultant other than to be sure that the PSAP management understands that it has been provided in the binder.
