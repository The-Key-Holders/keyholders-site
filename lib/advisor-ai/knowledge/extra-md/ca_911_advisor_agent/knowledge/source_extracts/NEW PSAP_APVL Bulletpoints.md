Below is a basic outline of what is required by the CA State 9-1-1 Branch for agency to become a New PSAP.

**Steps for becoming a New PSAP**

**Agency must meet specific criteria:**

* Reference the CA State 9-1-1 Operations Manual, Chapter III (Pg. 4-5): [CA 9-1-1 Operations Manual Chapter III Funding](https://www.caloes.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/PSC/Documents/004-ChapterIIIFUNDING2020.pdf)

**Submit agency letter of request, include:**

* Detailed description of current 911 call process
* Detailed evaluation summary of alternative arrangements reviewed
* Agency’s need to be recognized as a PSAP
* Agency confirmation to meet CA State 911 mandatory standards; including 24/7 for minimum of five-years and call-answer of 95% within 15-seconds
* Commitment to support/accept wireless 9-1-1 calls
* Commitment to support Text-to-911, becoming active within 12 months of going live
* Commitment to support Next Generation 9-1-1 services

**Letter Attachments:**

Detailed documentation to support 911 call-volume of 1,201+

* Copies of emergency incident reports / 9-1-1 call-volume report (last 18 months) showing agency activity from ECaTS Reporting Database; obtain from primary PSAP (Cathedral City PD).

**Emergency Number System Plan/Agreement (TDe280)**

* Complete TDe280 (CA State 911 Branch will provide)
* Obtain signatures from participating PSAPs that agree to send/receive 9-1-1 calls from requesting agency

**Establishing New PSAP**

* State will pay for “startup” funding for approved “new” PSAP.
* Funding is based on agency’s 911 call-volume at a P.01 grade of service (no more than one busy per 100 calls attempted during normal business hours).
* State pays quoted cost from vendor for system parts, labor and maintenance for five-year cycle.
* State pays for installation of required 9-1-1 circuits and monthly cost.

**Not State Supported**

State does not pay for workstations, chairs, headsets, other misc. items, only equipment required for first CPE System set-up

(future CPE Funding Allotments are based on new PSAP 911 call-volume from prior 18 months; funds remaining after CPE procured may then be used for residual 911 items)
