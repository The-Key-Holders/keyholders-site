**FUNDING PROCESS – 9‑1‑1 Network and Other 9‑1‑1 Services**

**Reimbursement Claim Process – 9‑1‑1 Network and Other 9‑1‑1 Services**

In accordance with the *9-1-1 EDUCATION FUNDING POLICY (pg. III.7)* and *9‑1‑1* *NETWORK SERVICES FUNDING POLICY (pgs. III.8-III.9)* outlined in this chapter, PSAPs requesting reimbursement funding for 9‑1‑1 network services and other 9‑1‑1 services (not GIS or CPE system replacement), may use the following Reimbursement Claim Process.

1. **PSAP Requests Funding**

The PSAP writes a letter on PSAP letterhead to the CA 9-1-1 Branch requesting the 9‑1‑1 service and/or equipment. The letter must include:

* 1. an overview of requirements with associated costs;
  2. procurement process PSAP plans on using to obtain equipment and services (i.e., competitive bid, noncompetitive bid, tariff, or State contract);
  3. desired installation date; and,
  4. the name of the PSAP’s finance administrator with authority to approve agency spending.

1. **CA 9-1-1 Branch Issues a Commitment To Fund**

If the PSAP funding request letter meets the requirements outlined in this chapter, a TDe**‑**288 form detailing the approved amount will be sent to the PSAP within two weeks of the date the letter was received by the CA 9-1-1 Branch. If the PSAP letter does not meet the funding requirements, the assigned CA 9-1-1 Branch consultant will contact the PSAP for further information within two weeks of the date the letter was received by the CA 9-1-1 Branch.

1. **The PSAP Purchases Services/Equipment**

The PSAP uses their procurement process and authorizes the contractor to provide and install the equipment according to the conditions of their contract.

1. **Contractor Invoices the PSAP**

Upon satisfactory installation of equipment or service, the contractor will invoice the PSAP.

1. **PSAP Submits Claim to the CA 9-1-1 Branch For Reimbursement**

Upon payment of the invoice, the PSAP may submit a REIMBURSEMENT CLAIM (TDe‑290) form to the CA 9-1-1 Branch, including the previously approved TDe‑288 with appropriate CA 9-1-1 Branch tracking number, a document confirming the PSAP’s acceptance date of the services (when applicable), and copies of paid invoices with proof of payment (e.g. cancelled check). The TDe-290 form may be viewed or downloaded from the CA 9-1-1 Branch website as follows:

<http://www.caloes.ca.gov/PSC/Pages/Publications/Forms.aspx> .

1. **Review, Approval, and Reimbursement**

Upon receipt of a complete and accurate TDe‑290 with appropriate documentation, the

CA 9-1-1 Branch will review and approve the request within two weeks. The claim will be forwarded to the Governor’s Office of Emergency Services, Accounting Branch, for payment processing. This final payment process may take up to 90 days.
