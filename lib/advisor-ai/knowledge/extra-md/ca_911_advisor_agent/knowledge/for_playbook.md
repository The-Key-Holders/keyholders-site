# FOR playbook - Fiscal and Operational Review

Source: `2024_FOR Prep.md`, older FOR prep instructions, design pack. Templates live under Advisor_Docs FOR folders on F:.

---

## Purpose

Structured review with PSAP management covering fiscal support, network, CPE, operational performance, and NG9-1-1/cloud readiness. Statewide cadence produces multiple FORs per month; each PSAP roughly on a multi-year cycle (often discussed as ~5 years).

---

## Binder structure (2024 instructions)

| Order | Section | Advisor prep notes |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| Cover | Cover sheet | PSAP name, review date |
| Front | **FOR Summary** | Write **last**; preliminary findings for discussion |
| I | **Fiscal Review** | Excel workbook: Summary, CPE, ATA, Reimbursement, Foreign Language; five-year estimate caveats |
| II | **Network Review** | Provider customer record; services list; billing accuracy; ETS correction path if BTN errors |
| III | **CPE and Maintenance** | TD-288 history; maintenance; residual narrative |
| IV | **Operational Performance** | 12-month answer times; TTY capability; ECaTS availability |
| V | **NG9-1-1 and Cloud-CPE** | Lab-validated options; funding Q&A |
| VI | **References** | Review reference list with PSAP |

---

## Fiscal workbook themes

- **Summary tab:** select PSAP; auto-rollups; adjust five-year estimate for one-time costs 
- **CPE tab:** last upgrade history: CPE+maint, extended maint, residual spending 
- **ATA tab:** current FY approved training expenditures 
- **Reimbursement tab:** non-ATA reimbursements past FY 
- **Foreign language:** Language Line usage/cost by PSAP 
- Network expenses tab may be limited depending on current data capture 

Include Summary + Foreign Language reports at minimum; add other tabs when issues exist.

---

## Operational standard to discuss

- At least **90% of 9-1-1 calls answered within 15 seconds** (training/Manual/NENA-aligned language) 
- Review roughly last **12 months** of ECaTS answer times 
- TTY: observe/auto-answer capability 
- Remind continuous ECaTS access for PSAP management 

---

## Agent outputs for FOR prep

1. Data collection todo (who to email for network record, Language Line, etc.) 
2. Section skeletons with fill-in prompts 
3. Meeting agenda (60–90 minutes) 
4. Neutral performance discussion points (no fabricated stats) 
5. Cross-link to FOR Assembly Engine if product exists
