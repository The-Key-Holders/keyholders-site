# Top 15 PSAP questions (talk tracks)

Source: Advisor Quick Reference Aid 05. Policy of record remains live Manual/forms.

| # | Question | Response sketch |
|---|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Are we eligible for a CPE replacement? | Generally **5 years from last TD-284**. Confirm date in ETS/PSAP file. Under 5: explain cycle. At/over 5: guide Advanced Notification. |
| 2 | How much funding will we get? | Based on **ECaTS call volume** + fixed allotment methodology. Calculator = estimate. **Official = Branch allotment letter.** |
| 3 | CPE vs network funding? | **CPE** = call-handling system. **Network/NG9-1-1** = call delivery path. Separate contracts/tracks. |
| 4 | Cloud or on-premise? | Both may be available via **lab-validated MPA** vendors. Cloud often NG/i3 friendly. Encourage demos; no brand mandate. |
| 5 | What is Advanced Notification / when due? | Intent form for CPE funds. Typically up to **~1 year before** need/eligibility. Supports budgeting; missing it can delay. |
| 6 | What is residual funding? | Allotment minus complete system cost (if positive). **Approved list only**; plan with project; quotes often within **90 days** of acceptance. |
| 7 | Residuals for furniture/chairs/headsets? | Often yes **if on current list** and in 9-1-1 call-taking workspace; chair/furniture limits apply. Confirm list. |
| 8 | Who pays the vendor? | **Direct:** contractor invoices Branch after TD-284 path. **Reimbursement:** PSAP pays then TD-290. |
| 9 | What is TD-288? | **Commitment to Fund.** Required before authorized spend; tracking # on invoices. |
| 10 | What is TD-284? | **System Acceptance.** Triggers payment path and residual clocks. |
| 11 | Maintenance after 5 years? | Years **6–7** extended maint may be fundable with pre-approval (confirm current). Year **8+** often PSAP cost. Cloud may continue commercially; refresh still residual-relevant. |
| 12 | Damaged CPE; will State pay? | Negligence/poor facility → usually PSAP. Act of nature + reasonable protection → **case-by-case**. |
| 13 | Must we use MPA? | Not always mandatory, but enables **direct funding** and max-price shopping. Local buy often → reimbursement path. |
| 14 | NG9-1-1 effect on CPE choice? | Prefer lab-validated **NG-capable/i3-friendly** systems; coordinate cutovers. |
| 15 | Training money (ATA)? | **Separate** from CPE. Pre-approval; different claim routing. Do not mix into residual without policy basis. |

## Call close checklist

1. Restate next action  
2. Note PSAP code, date, commitment in working log  
3. Send follow-up with form links / caloes.ca.gov/911
