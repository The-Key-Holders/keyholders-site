# PB01 - Eligibility and Advance Notification

## Goal
Determine CPE replacement funding window and first submission.

## Inputs
- Last TD-284 acceptance date (if known)
- Need-by timing
- County + PSAP identity

## Steps
1. Explain general **5-year cycle** from last acceptance (confirm Manual).
2. If under 5 years: explain cycle; mention years 6–7 extended maint pre-approval themes; year 8+ often PSAP cost.
3. If at/over 5 or approaching: guide **Advance Notification for CPE Funding**.
4. Timing: typically up to ~1 year before eligibility/need.
5. Explain next Branch step: **CPE Fixed Allotment letter** (timing per current practice).

## Outputs
- Eligibility narrative
- Form name + public forms pointer
- Draft email T-01 style

## Escalate
Damage, early replacement, consolidations with unclear acceptance date.
