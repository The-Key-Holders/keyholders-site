# PSAP Portal process digest (RFP 26-16743)

## Advance Notification (Attachment 11 themes)
- Standardized form to Branch/county Advisor before CPE replacement funding.
- Explicit Cloud Solution vs On-Premises Solution selection.
- Fiscal Year identification.
- Purpose: time for PSAPs to evaluate contractors in the pool; Branch budgeting.
- Submit roughly up to one year before eligibility/need (Chapter III practice).

## SOW (Attachment 16 themes)
Expected sections:
- Equipment lists: quantities, hardware prices, monthly maintenance costs
- Design/network: NG9-1-1 circuits, gateways, UPS, logging, CAD/radio/time sync integrations
- Change requests: PSAP + CA 9-1-1 Branch approval
- Installation schedule tied to funding approval / TD-288 issuance
- Training plan, maintenance plan, responsibilities (including Branch)
- Appendices: site certification, floor plans (critical for on-prem)
- Cloud emphasis: bandwidth, data center connectivity, remote support
- Rule: do not deploy both cloud and on-prem cores at a single PSAP

## Invoice (Attachments 14/15 themes)
Mandatory themes:
- Contract/Tracking No. referencing TDe-288
- One-time charges broken out (Equipment, Labor, Others, Taxes/Surcharges)
- Separate monthly maintenance amount and term dates
- System Acceptance Date
- PSAP identifiers: County Code, PSAP Number, Service Number, Vendor Abbreviation
- Total of approved TD-288 reference; invoice should not exceed approved amount
- Route per Branch instructions (often CA911Invoicing@caloes.ca.gov)

## TD-288 / TD-284
- TD-288 Commitment to Fund before authorized spend on direct path
- TD-284 System Acceptance enables payment path and residual clocks
- New SOW: install ready for AT within 180 days of TD-288 unless mutually revised

## Residuals
- Allotment minus complete system cost
- Approved residual list only; plan with project (TDe-285)
- Common windows: quotes/POs ~90 days post acceptance; claims often within 12 months (confirm current policy)

## Vendor pool
- Direct SETNA path uses awarded MPA contractors after RFP 26-16743 award
- Historical 2020 MPA price sheets archive-only for new projects
- Evaluate multiple comparable quotes within the pool
