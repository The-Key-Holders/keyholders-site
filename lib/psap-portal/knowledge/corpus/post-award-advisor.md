# Post-award Advisor operations (Sept 2026+)

## Flow
Advance Notification (E1) → Allotment + TD e-288 → Vendor SOW (E2) → SOW approval → TD e-288 link → Execution + status (P6) → Change orders (E3/P4) → Install/TD284 (P7) → Invoice (P5) + Training (P9) → MACs (P8) → Closeout (P10).

## Top pain points
1 SOW compliance 2 Invoice rejects 3 Change order bottleneck 4 Incomplete Adv Notice 5 Cloud vs On-Prem confusion 6 TD e-288 backlog 7 PSAP sign-off delays.

## Mitigations via portal
SOW checker, invoice checker, Adv Notice wizard, cloud vs on-prem, TD-288 package checker, vendor pool, structured questions, news, role-based Advisor desk with process map and request catalog.

## SLAs (typical)
Adv Notice ack 1-2d decision 5-10d; SOW initial 3-5d full 7-15d; CO simple 3-7d complex 7-14d; invoice triage 1-2d verify 3-7d.

## Key docs
Att 11 Adv Notice, TD e-288, Att 16 SOW, TD284, Att 14/15 invoice, Change Order, Att 10 confidentiality.
