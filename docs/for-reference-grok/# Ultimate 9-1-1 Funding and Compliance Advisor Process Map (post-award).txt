# Ultimate 9-1-1 Funding/Compliance Advisor Process Map
**Post-Award Phase | RFP 26-16743 | Effective September 2026+**

**Purpose**: This single integrated process map merges all internal processes, external/internal requests, pain points, mitigation strategies, tasks/sub-tasks, timelines, and forms into one cohesive operational framework for CA 9-1-1 Branch County Funding/Compliance Advisors.

---

## High-Level Process Flow Overview
PSAP submits Advance Notification (E1)
↓
Process 1: Funding Notification & Allotment (TD e-288 created)
↓
PSAP engages Awarded Vendor → Draft SOW submitted (E2)
↓
Process 2: SOW Review & Approval
↓
Process 3: TD e-288 Finalization / Linking
↓
Project Execution (ongoing Process 6: Status Reporting)
↓
Change Orders as needed (E3 → Process 4)
↓
Installation → Testing → Acceptance (Process 7: TD284)
↓
Invoicing (Process 5) + Training (Process 9)
↓
Post-Acceptance MACs (Process 8)
↓
Process 10: Project Closeout & Archiving



**Key Cross-Cutting Elements**:
- **Central Documents**: Advance Notification (Att. 11), TD e-288, SOW (Att. 16), TD284, Invoice Template (Att. 14/15)
- **Highest Volume Pain Points**: SOW compliance, Invoice rejections, Change order delays, TD e-288 backlog
- **Primary Triggers**: External Requests from PSAPs/Vendors

## Phase 1: Funding Initiation

### Process 1: Advance CPE Funding Notification Processing & Allotment Issuance

**Description**: Receive, review, and process the standardized Advance Notification form to determine and issue funding allotment via TD e-288.

**Primary Trigger**:
- **External Request E1**: PSAP submits completed Advance Notification for CPE Funding form (Attachment 11) — Cloud or On-Prem selection.

**Key Tasks & Sub-Tasks**:
1. **Intake & Logging**
   - Receive form via email from PSAP
   - Log into internal tracker with date/time, PSAP name, County, solution type (Cloud/On-Prem)
2. **Completeness & Eligibility Review**
   - Verify all required fields completed
   - Confirm PSAP eligibility and fiscal year alignment
   - Validate Cloud vs On-Prem selection is clear
3. **Allotment Determination**
   - Calculate/review allotment amount per funding guidelines
   - Identify any conditions or restrictions
4. **TD e-288 Creation**
   - Create new TD e-288 record
   - Link to PSAP and fiscal year
   - Notify PSAP of allotment decision and next steps (vendor evaluation window)
5. **Documentation & Handoff**
   - Update county tracker
   - Send formal notification to PSAP with TD e-288 reference

**Pain Points & Mitigation Strategies** (where they occur in this process):

| Pain Point | Location in Process | Mitigation Strategy (Critical Elements) |
|------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| **4. Incomplete/Non-Compliant Advance Notification Forms** | Intake & Review | • Use fillable PDF with required-field indicators<br>• Provide one-pager "How to Complete" guide with examples<br>• Add quick admin completeness check before advisor review<br>• Include Cloud vs On-Prem decision guidance attachment |
| **11. Initial Workload Spike** | Entire process (first 3-6 months) | • Implement admin triage for completeness<br>• Set realistic turnaround SLAs and communicate them<br>• Temporarily surge resources for first 90-120 days<br>• Prioritize by fiscal urgency |
| **5. Cloud vs On-Prem Confusion** | Allotment Determination | • Attach one-page Cloud vs On-Prem comparison matrix<br>• Use standardized decision-support questions |

**Key Forms & Documents**:
- Advance Notification for CPE Funding (Attachment 11)
- TD e-288 (new record creation)
- Internal Allotment Log / County Tracker

**Typical Timeline**:
- Acknowledgment: 1-2 business days
- Full review + allotment decision: 5-10 business days
- TD e-288 creation: Same day as decision

## Phase 2: Project Planning & Approval

### Process 2: SOW Review, Coordination & Approval

**Description**: Review draft Statement of Work from vendor/PSAP against Attachment 16 template for completeness, pricing alignment, and technical soundness.

**Primary Triggers**:
- **External Request E2**: PSAP or Vendor submits draft SOW for review/approval.

**Key Tasks & Sub-Tasks**:
1. **Initial Receipt & Logging**
2. **Completeness Check** (against all 13 subsections of Attachment 16)
3. **Pricing & Scope Validation** (cross-check with awarded Exhibit C rates)
4. **Technical/Integration Review** (coordinate with NG9-1-1 team if needed)
5. **Comments / Approval Issuance**
6. **Link to TD e-288** and update project tracker

**Pain Points & Mitigation Strategies**:

| Pain Point | Location in Process | Mitigation Strategy (Critical Elements) |
|------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| **1. SOW Template Compliance Issues** | Completeness Check | • Mandate 1-page SOW Completeness Checklist<br>• Provide 2-3 pre-filled example SOWs (Cloud/On-Prem)<br>• Mandatory short vendor training within 30 days of award<br>• Offer optional pre-submission review |
| **8. Pricing/Scope Misalignment** | Pricing Validation | • Require reference to specific Exhibit C line items<br>• Add quick internal cross-check step<br>• Return non-compliant SOWs within 48 hours |
| **10. Integration Gaps Discovered Late** | Technical Review | • Require early "Integration Discovery Workshop"<br>• Document known limitations in SOW<br>• Build library of common integration issues/solutions |
| **16. New Vendors Unfamiliar with Processes** | Entire process | • Include "Working with Cal OES" orientation in vendor onboarding<br>• Assign primary advisor contact for first 3-6 months |

**Key Forms & Documents**:
- Sample SOW Template (Attachment 16)
- Vendor draft SOW + detailed quote (from Exhibit C)
- SOW Completeness Checklist (new internal tool)
- TD e-288 reference

**Typical Timeline**:
- Initial review: 3-5 business days
- Full coordination & approval: 7-15 business days

### Process 3: TD e-288 Management, Tracking & Amendment

**Description**: Create, maintain, amend, and reconcile the central funding authorization/tracking document (TD e-288).

**Key Tasks & Sub-Tasks**:
1. Create initial TD e-288 upon allotment
2. Link to specific PSAP project and vendor
3. Monitor spend vs approved total
4. Process amendments (especially from approved change orders)
5. Provide status updates to PSAP/Vendor upon request
6. Reconcile with paid invoices during closeout

**Pain Points & Mitigation Strategies**:

| Pain Point | Location in Process | Mitigation Strategy (Critical Elements) |
|------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| **6. TD e-288 Backlog** | Creation & Amendment | • Standardized intake form with required fields<br>• Shared visible TD e-288 Tracker with aging<br>• Internal SLAs (new = 2 days, amendments = 3 days)<br>• Pre-populate data from Advance Notification |
| **3. Change Order Bottleneck** | Amendment step | • Use standardized Change Order Form<br>• Define approval thresholds for expedited processing<br>• Maintain shared Change Order Tracker |

**Key Forms & Documents**:
- TD e-288 (core document)
- Change Order documentation
- Invoice copies (for reconciliation)

**Typical Timeline**:
- Initial creation: Same day as allotment decision
- Amendments: 2-5 business days after change order approval

## Phase 3: Execution & Change Management

### Process 4: Change Order Review & CA 9-1-1 Branch Approval

**Description**: Review, assess impact, and formally approve (or reject) changes to scope, cost, or timeline.

**Primary Trigger**:
- **External Request E3**: Vendor submits Change Order Request (requires Branch approval).

**Key Tasks & Sub-Tasks**:
1. Receive and log Change Order Request
2. Assess impact on scope, cost, timeline, and funding
3. Coordinate review/concurrence with PSAP
4. Approve, reject, or negotiate
5. Update TD e-288 if additional funding required
6. Distribute approved document to all parties

**Pain Points & Mitigation Strategies**:

| Pain Point | Location in Process | Mitigation Strategy (Critical Elements) |
|------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| **3. Change Order Bottleneck** | Entire process | • Standardized Change Order Form with impact fields<br>• Define clear approval thresholds (<5% cost = expedited)<br>• Shared tracker with aging + published 48-72hr SLA<br>• Train advisors on fast decision frameworks |
| **12. Unclear Escalation Paths** | Dispute resolution | • Create 3-step escalation matrix with timeframes<br>• Document common dispute types + resolution approaches |
| **13. Post-Acceptance MAC Scope Creep** | Classification step | • Define clear Minor MAC vs Major Change criteria<br>• Create MAC Request Log reviewed monthly |

**Key Forms & Documents**:
- Vendor Change Order Form + Branch approval record
- Original approved SOW
- Updated TD e-288 (if funding changes)

**Typical Timeline**:
- Simple changes: 3-7 business days
- Complex changes: 7-14 business days

### Process 6: Project Timeline Monitoring & Status Reporting (Cross-Cutting)

**Description**: Maintain accurate project status across all active CPE projects and respond to internal/external status requests.

**Key Tasks & Sub-Tasks**:
1. Maintain/update county-level project tracker
2. Flag risks and delays
3. Produce internal status reports for supervisor/leadership
4. Respond to ad-hoc status requests from PSAPs or other branches
5. Escalate systemic issues

**Pain Points & Mitigation Strategies**:

| Pain Point | Location in Process | Mitigation Strategy (Critical Elements) |
|------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| **15. Maintaining Accurate Real-Time Tracking** | Daily/Weekly updates | • Standardize county tracker template with weekly update cadence<br>• Assign data steward responsibility + monthly quality checks<br>• Explore low-code dashboard tools |
| **11. Workload Spike** | High-volume periods | • Prioritize by urgency<br>• Use surge capacity when needed |

**Key Forms & Documents**:
- Internal Project Status Tracker / Dashboard
- Monthly Status Report template

## Phase 4: Delivery, Acceptance & Closeout

### Process 5: Invoice Verification, Triage & Payment Coordination

**Description**: Review invoices for compliance with template and approved funding before payment.

**Pain Points & Mitigation Strategies** (heavily concentrated here):

| Pain Point | Location in Process | Mitigation Strategy (Critical Elements) |
|------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| **2. Invoice Rejections & Payment Delays** | Verification step | • One-page Invoice Quick Reference Guide for vendors<br>• Internal Invoice Validation Checklist<br>• Optional pre-submission review<br>• Weekly "Top 5 Mistakes" summaries to vendors |
| **6. TD e-288 Reconciliation** | Cross-check step | • Use shared TD e-288 Tracker<br>• Reconcile remaining balance before approval |

**Key Forms & Documents**:
- CPE Service Invoice Template (Attachment 15)
- Invoice Instructions (Attachment 14)
- TD e-288
- Approved SOW + Change Orders
- TD284 (for final payments)

**Typical Timeline**:
- Initial triage: 1-2 business days
- Verification & issue resolution: 3-7 business days

---

### Process 7: Acceptance Testing Coordination (TD284)

**Description**: Ensure proper use of TD284 checklist and formal acceptance sign-off.

**Pain Points**:
- Sign-off delays (Pain Point 7)
- Integration issues surfacing during testing (Pain Point 10)

**Mitigation**: Include "Recommended Review Timeline" in SOW; require early integration workshops; track average sign-off time by county.

**Key Forms**:
- System Acceptance Checklist (TD284)
- Formal Acceptance Sign-off

---

### Process 8: Post-Acceptance MACs Handling

**Description**: Manage Moves/Adds/Changes after formal acceptance.

**Pain Points**:
- **13. MAC Scope Creep** — Mitigate with clear Minor vs Major criteria and MAC Request Log.

---

### Process 9: Training Coordination & Documentation

**Description**: Ensure training is delivered and properly documented per SOW section k.

**Pain Points**:
- **14. Training Documentation Gaps** — Mitigate with mandatory Training Completion & Sign-off Form + 5-day submission requirement.

---

### Process 10: Project Closeout, Archiving & Compliance Preparation

**Description**: Final verification that all deliverables are complete and project file is audit-ready.

**Key Tasks**:
1. Verify all deliverables complete
2. Confirm final invoice paid and reconciled
3. Collect and archive complete project file
4. Update county history/master tracker
5. Prepare for audits
6. Close TD e-288 as "Complete"

**Pain Points & Mitigation Strategies**:

| Pain Point | Location in Process | Mitigation Strategy (Critical Elements) |
|------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| **18. Audit Trail & Record Completeness Gaps** | Archiving step | • Standardized Project File Checklist<br>• Random 10% file audits in year one<br>• Require "file complete" sign-off before closing project |
| **19. Confidentiality Compliance** | Onboarding & closeout | • Require signed statements before new personnel start<br>• Maintain central log per project |

**Key Forms & Documents**:
- Full Project File Checklist
- Final Invoice + Payment Record
- TD284 Acceptance
- Closeout Checklist (internal)
- Archive index / retention log
