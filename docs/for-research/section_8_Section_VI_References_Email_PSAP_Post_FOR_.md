The NENA EPRC is now available using the RapidDeploy’s mapping solution and can be accessed at any PSAP on the RapidDeploy platform.  Some information in EPRC is outdated or incorrect.  CalOES is now requiring PSAP’s log into the portal, at a minimum, twice yearly, or when there is a new contact to update and ensure the information is accurate.  Please use the link provided to make sure the information for your PSAP is up to date and accurate.https://www.caloes.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/PSC/Documents/CA-9-1-1-Branch-Notice-2023-109-ENHANCED-PSAP-REGISTRY-AND-CENSUS.pdfNorthern911 is a Canadian based company that offers support to PSAPs if they receive a 9-1-1 call from outside their jurisdiction and require assistance in identifying the PSAP and phone number to transfer the call. . VoIP calls that don’t come through the 911 network will default to Northern911.  PSAPs can call the Northern911 Emergency Response Centre at 1-866-869-9959 and request assistance in identifying the PSAP and phone number to that PSAP for calls received outside their jurisdiction/county/state and within North America.Northern911 – Information:  http://www.northern911.com/911-services/psap-support/Note: A charge is associated with this service; confirm process with Northern911
