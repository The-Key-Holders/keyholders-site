9-1-1 call handling equipment.  The web-based client service simply allows two call-takers to securely login from a web browser.  Alternately, the integrated function requires the PSAP call handling equipment to be capable of receiving SMS text.  Please consult with your equipment vendor to determine system capabilities for SMS text integration.The CA 9-1-1 Branch will fund a maximum of was August 1, 2018.  Please see the California 911 Operations manual Chapter X regarding the further details on the Text to 911 Deployment at:
